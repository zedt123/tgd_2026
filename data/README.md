## Datasets

This repository uses small, subsets of standard image datasets
for non-commercial research and evaluation purposes.

### FFHQ
We use a validation subset of the FFHQ dataset provided by the authors of the
DAPS paper:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RHNif32W0hvB4M75ppG1ypTChy-W3q3Z

From this subset, we use only 100 images for evaluation purposes.
The original FFHQ dataset was curated by NVIDIA Corporation.
The FFHQ dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) license.

This repository includes a non-commercial subset of 100 FFHQ images for
research and evaluation purposes only. Users must comply with the FFHQ license
terms and provide appropriate attribution.

### ImageNet
ImageNet images are **not redistributed** in this repository due to licensing
restrictions.

ImageNet is available for non-commercial research and educational use only,
subject to the ImageNet license agreement:
https://www.image-net.org/

In our experiments, we used a subset of 100 ImageNet images 
(that weren't used for training) for evaluation.
Users who wish to reproduce these results must obtain ImageNet themselves and
agree to the ImageNet terms and conditions.
